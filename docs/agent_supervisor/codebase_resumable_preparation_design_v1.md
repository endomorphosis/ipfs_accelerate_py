Proposed resumable CodebaseIR preparation, version 1.

Status: design proposal dated 2026-10-12. The preparation records and APIs below are not implemented or qualified. This proposal preserves the existing complete-head publication rules and adds recoverable work before publication. Structural analysis, learned representations, checker evidence and worker admission retain separate authority.

`RepositoryCodebaseIndex._prepare` currently captures one complete `RepositorySnapshot`, retains its captured source map, invokes `RepositoryScanner.scan_snapshot`, seals all source/AST/manifest artifacts and publishes one complete AST batch. `prepare_current` then reobserves the whole repository before the catalog transaction. Cancellation is checked between stages and files; there is no durable preparation cursor. The default preparation profile is 256 entries at 64 KiB per file, with an admission estimate based on the full source envelope.

`codebase_inventory_resume.start_current_codebase_scan` begins with an already published complete structural head and an already registered model. Its root/page/cursor records resume numerical inventory work, not interrupted source capture or structural preparation. Its current profile admits at most 1,024 inventory entries, 64 entries per page, a 4 MiB manifest, 32 MiB worker input and 16 MiB output. Pure cursor tests exercise chain integrity; the 257-member lineage fixture supplies native target preparation, not a preparation restart. The separately qualified full-300 campaign resumes numerical pages against a complete head. These provide a reference for a new preparation protocol without supplying its missing qualification.

There are useful existing foundations. `semantic_index/chunked_snapshot.py` and `paged_snapshot.py` represent complete committed-Git inventories and bounded content/evidence blocks. `streaming_scanner.py` analyzes one ordinary file in an owned child and deterministically assembles pytest identities and the symbol graph. They require an adapter: committed blobs do not represent dirty or untracked worktree contents; paged evidence does not satisfy the current manifest's exact full-snapshot evidence envelope; and a permitted 32 MiB semantic state can exceed the inventory resume profile's 4 MiB manifest ceiling. The ordinary `_prepare` path continues to rederive untrusted previous semantics. Although `RepositoryScanner.scan_snapshot(previous_state=...)` supports reuse, a caller's matching source CID and extractor labels do not independently authenticate those prior facts.

The proposed first profile preserves the current worktree acquisition policy, exclusions, raw path identity and source-byte checks. Committed-only acquisition remains an explicitly different profile. Start with at most 1,024 entries, 64 KiB captured per ordinary source and 16-entry pages, allowing a separately qualified 64-entry page configuration. Root, page and final paged-manifest envelopes each stay within 4 MiB. Each source's normalized semantic facts occupy one artifact of at most 1 MiB; oversized facts receive an explicit refusal or unknown disposition under the chosen policy. Global assembly retains a prospective aggregate/count budget, initially no larger than the streaming scanner's 32 MiB fact ceiling and 100,000-record ceiling. These are proposed representation limits, not an increase to existing worker, artifact-store, scheduler or finite-close caps. Owners must admit actual aggregate input, output, transient retention and process limits before running a profile.

The root records a complete freshly captured inventory before analysis begins. Source bytes are sealed as independent RAW CIDv1 artifacts, using bounded sequential capture rather than retaining a growing byte dictionary. Acquisition must reobserve the complete membership and source identities before returning the root. The root may reference bounded inventory pages; it must account for every path, including opaque, unsupported and failed inputs. It cannot turn an ordinary source opaque merely to avoid a retention limit. A captured source hash proves content identity, not analysis or formal correctness.

Proposed closed schemas use exact types and reject unknown fields. Metadata and float-free AST/fact structures use the existing canonical DAG-JSON domain. Source bytes use RAW. Projection clocks are retained as canonical finite hexadecimal strings and restored exactly. Numerical artifacts containing floating-point rows keep their separately declared RAW protocol; they are never retagged as structural DAG-JSON.

| `codebase-preparation-completion@1` | Root CID; full ordered page descriptors; coverage/disposition totals; assembled semantic-index page descriptors; final manifest CID; expected head; authority/policy flags | Entire inventory partition and artifact closure verified, deterministic assembly finished and closing source/head/context fences passed |
| Proposed `codebase-ir-structural-manifest@2` | Complete source and unit inventories through bounded pages; exact snapshot acquisition evidence; ordered semantic-index references; coverage/limitations; AST revision identity | Explicit loader and catalog validation for every referenced page; bounded manifest envelope does not imply its transitive bodies were checked |

Candidate APIs are `start_codebase_preparation`, `prepare_codebase_page`, `validate_codebase_preparation_prefix` and `complete_codebase_preparation`. The ordinary APIs remain available with unchanged acceptance rules. Once qualified, preparation reuse should default on with an exact-boolean opt-out that executes the complete reference route. A private operation may reuse immutable context or already checked canonical facts; a public receiver never accepts a caller's prevalidated Boolean, offset or digest as a replacement for native validation.

Each page reads and hashes its exact captured source body, verifies the root's parser/extractor context, derives source-local AST and semantic facts, seals every referenced artifact, and atomically publishes the complete page last. A failed page leaves at most unreferenced immutable objects. It advances no cursor, changes no active AST revision or head, and contributes no completed member. The page is an inventory outcome ledger, so unsupported language, parse failure, opacity and explicit budget refusal remain visible. Complete inventory coverage and complete positive analysis are separate fields. A policy requiring all analysis must refuse publication while any relevant member remains deferred or unknown.

Durable pages preserve projection timestamps rather than inventing fresh clocks at each restart. Source-local semantic facts retain their original identities and provenance. On restart a fresh owner validates the root, complete prefix, artifact bodies and extractor context before using a page; it also freshly reobserves the current repository. Dirty source, additions, removals, renames, changed exclusions, changed parser options or a changed expected head invalidate the current preparation attempt. A successor starts a distinct root. Independently admitted unchanged source shards may be rebound through the existing canonical source/context mechanism, but an old-root page or cursor cannot be relabeled as a successor page. Exact operation retries retain their existing historical receipt semantics and do not establish present currentness.

Global assembly runs only after the complete source-local partition verifies. Feed bounded per-file fact bytes into the existing pytest identity unification, configuration/lock-edge logic and symbol-graph resolver in deterministic inventory order. Resolve cross-file calls, fixtures, imports and edges against the whole inventory. Do not concatenate already resolved page graphs or assume a page is semantically independent. Persist bounded symbol, artifact, edge and limitation index pages; their descriptors bind exact counts, ordering, schema/context and CIDs. Test complete reconstructed state and all graph identities against the existing monolithic scanner, including cross-page pytest fixtures and ambiguous call targets, before comparing hashes.

Final publication verifies every preparation/index page and the complete native AST membership under bounded streaming or explicitly admitted batch validation. It performs a fresh full source fence, checks the expected head again, and commits the final structural head, complete AST revision, supersession and operation receipt in the catalog owner's transaction. The publication retains cancellation/deadline hooks, complete-head CAS, replay/conflict behavior and rollback. No partial page can publish a normal head. CAS objects may survive a failed final transaction as unreferenced artifacts. A successful completion remains a bounded source observation; later planning must reobserve currentness rather than assume a filesystem lock or continuous validity.

The first native preparation campaign should use an authored 34-or-more-file repository with at least three 16-entry pages, then a complete 300-member comparison before any larger profile. Required controls include:

- A genuine new interpreter after one completed page; unchanged prefix reuse; interruption before page sealing, between artifact/page publication and after final artifact creation; no cursor advance from a partial page.
- Addition, deletion, edit and rename successors across page boundaries; dirty tracked and untracked files; opaque/raw-name inputs, unsupported languages and failed/partial parses; exact full inventory and explicit unknown dispositions.
- Missing, reordered, duplicated, cyclic, foreign or corrupted pages; forged cursor offsets; changed later source bytes, context fingerprints or options; externally anchored CIDs whose bodies are absent, resealed or noncanonical.
- Cancellation/deadline checks during capture, per-file analysis, prefix replay, global assembly, source closing and late catalog commit; byte-identical table/head/receipt/counter rollback and cleanup of actual owned children.
- Exact complete AST/projection, semantic state, thirteen-table, graph/index and receipt parity with the monolithic reference route; clocks, invalidations and operation generations included. Compare full objects before hashes.
- Source-file, semantic-fact, inventory/page, transitive-manifest, global-record and aggregate-retention boundaries; empty input and the maximum admitted population. Refusals preserve required task coverage instead of silently dropping members.

Measure capture, per-file extraction, global assembly, artifact validation, prefix replay and final commit separately. Existing numerical resume walks and replays the saved prefix on each page and reobserves the whole source at entry and exit; that work can grow with page count. First record the work and end-to-end cost. Any later incremental verification must preserve complete membership and fresh-source guarantees. A CID tree can reduce repeated serialization or identify changed immutable blocks; it cannot substitute metadata/mtime claims for required source-byte verification. Preserve independent opt-outs and do not infer general throughput from small fixtures.

The structural index feeds the planner through source-bound units, graph facts and explicit limitations. Autoencoders may propose candidate formalizations and match IntentIR against the codebase generation. A proof cache/index must separately bind an obligation CID, exact source/dependency generation, formalization/lowering version, checker/policy identity and independently accepted checker result artifact. Learned similarity, a reconstructed statement and an unindexed or opaque file do not discharge an obligation. The symbolic planner may rank these inputs while retaining every required acceptance task and unknown frontier.

Numerical CodebaseIR scans and on-demand training remain separate from preparation. Qualify 8D, 384D, 768D and Leanstral 4096D routes individually with declared source/latent dimensions, tokenizer/encoder and feature-codec contracts, exact source spans, complete parameter and optimizer state, lineage, and independent train/tune/canary roles. The existing structural latent-width-8 fixture does not qualify semantic 8D or any other head. Each CUDA route needs actual admitted device placement, CPU/reference equivalence, cancellation and memory observations. Pure source preparation performs no model forward, fitting or GPU allocation.

Implement the remaining milestones in this order:

1. Add pure closed-schema/CID/prefix controls and the versioned paged-manifest loader. Adapt worktree capture and source-local extraction while retaining the committed-only distinction and untrusted-previous safeguards.
2. Implement durable preparation pages, deterministic global assembly and the final catalog transaction. Qualify real restart, the 34-plus successor/refusal controls, then complete 300-member default/reference parity and bounded retention.
3. Integrate supervisor worker receiving and signed preparation dispatch. Bind an admitted issuer/task profile, root/expected-head/context, artifact closure, resource limits and operation/round identity; revalidate in the receiving owner. Signing an advisory page cannot grant formal proof or head-publication authority. Qualify installation, reservation, start, cancellation, completion, stop and cold receiving separately.
4. Connect source-bound candidate formalizations to accepted checker evidence and the planner's proof cache/index, with invalidation on exact source/dependency/context changes. Then qualify per-head numerical/CUDA scans and on-demand training under their own profiles.
5. Federate compatible updates into one model per lineage and head, with explicit round, parent-model, task/codec/source compatibility, contributor identity, aggregation and optimizer semantics. Keep independent lineages separate. Define a later versioned gradient-sync request/result contract for `ipfs_accelerate_py` and `mcp-plus-plus`, including step/state identities, reduction policy, duplicate/retry handling, deadlines, cancellation and stale-round refusal. This proposal starts no collective, remote trainer or gradient transport.

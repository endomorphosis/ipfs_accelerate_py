# Full 300 source preview and compact worker contract plan

This plan extends the native preview-bound successor work to the complete 300-source fixture while keeping task storage bounded. First qualify a genuine full-population preview and signed companion with sufficient reserved memory. Then add separately versioned artifact references before installing or launching the full-population worker. Each milestone requires its own evidence and acceptance decision.

## Current evidence and limits

The fresh 32-source native attempt 03 failed during retirement and does not count as successful worker qualification. Attempt 04 qualified the fresh CPU8D fixture: its [native result](../../../../artifacts/codebase_ir_terminal_bench/preview-successor-worker-native-20261005-04/native/result.json) records all 32 entries across four default and four opt-out pages, two fresh-process continuations, two explicit setup epochs, zero subsequent fitting or receiving-time inference attempts, and both authored TYPE/FORMAT tasks completed through actual public checks and publication merge. STOP left zero members and final resource cleanup verified; cancellation covered pre-cancelled admission and cancellation after completion before STOP, without claiming cancellation during a running task. The [container receipt](../../../../artifacts/codebase_ir_terminal_bench/preview-successor-worker-native-20261005-04/container-execution-final.json) records qualification, container removal and host reservation release. Full-300 execution, new-generation reproof and larger-head/CUDA qualification remain open.

The existing [worker profile](codebase_preview_successor_worker_qualification.md) embeds an immutable version 6 admission in a version 7 child manifest. Pending version 3 also embeds the child manifest, complete graph and signed planning receipt. The compact planning receipt reference alone does not make task bodies compact.

The [retained 300-source parent admission](../../../../artifacts/codebase_ir_terminal_bench/signed-successor-worker-qualification-20261003-08/native/admission.json) is 626,143 raw bytes and 626,142 bytes when serialized with compact separators, UTF-8 text and finite JSON values. Its existing encoding is already compact plus one newline. The [Intent repository](../../ipfs_accelerate_py/agent_supervisor/task_sources/intent_repository.py) limits bodies to 262,144 bytes. The embedded parent alone exceeds that cap; raising preview memory or removing indentation cannot admit this worker population.

## 1 Derive the preview reservation from the capture limits

Update the [native preview helper](../../benchmarks/agent_supervisor/container_coding/preview_successor_native_profile.py) to derive a preparation reservation from the original captured limits:

```text
preview_memory_mb = max(256, ceil(16 * max_entries * max_file_bytes / 1048576))
```

Use exact positive integers and the native `CodebaseScanLimits.validate_reservation` check. Reject a requirement above the caller's authorized envelope or the existing 4,096 MiB helper ceiling. The retained 512-entry, 65,536-byte capture envelope requires 512 MiB. The 64-entry envelope used for the smaller fixture fits the current 256 MiB preview reservation.

Reserve the same derived amount in the preview orchestration lease, `RepositoryPlanPreviewOwner` and every independent structural/root observation. Later receiving operations acquire their own bounded resources after the initial preview lease has closed. Keep deadlines, cancellation, existing MAIN pool configuration and native receiving checks. This calculation is an admission estimate; report actual memory observations separately.

Acceptance checks:

- The original 300-source population and its capture limits survive unchanged; no sampling or source-limit reduction substitutes for capacity.
- Insufficient envelopes reject before native preparation; no independent scheduler or relaxed resource policy appears.
- Fresh root/checker observers reread current source, persisted policies and validation script bytes on every use.
- The reservation covers the actual nested observations and releases on success, cancellation and preparation failure.

## 2 Qualify the complete preview and signed companion first

Run a fresh bounded full-300 source/model setup, select the complete successor and construct the genuine typed request, materials and preview through native owners. Keep the parent version 6 admission immutable. Author and receive the signed companion against that parent, exact source/model heads and complete independently authored task/acceptance population.

Persist the request, materials identities, preview, signed companion, immutable parent and independent root/checker observations. Record source and model generations, all artifact identities, finite resource envelopes and final cleanup. Include a standalone reader of the retained byte closure.

Acceptance checks:

- All 300 selected entries are present, with exact declared creations, task dependencies, validations, output constraints and acceptance mappings.
- Source, model, policy or checker drift rejects receiving; the matching score cannot remove an authored requirement.
- Full companion replay and fresh native closing succeed under the derived reservation, and owned work drains before lease release.
- Report this as full-300 preview/companion qualification. It provides no task-installation, dispatch, START or completion credit.

## 3 Add new closed compact worker profiles

Introduce new manifest, pending, receipt/reference and public-instruction versions together. Treat version 8 manifest and version 4 pending as proposed assignments, subject to the final schema review. Existing version 7 and pending version 3 retain their exact inline formats and validators.

A proposed artifact reference has exactly `schema`, `artifact_schema`, `codec`, `cid`, `sha256`, `bytes` and `store_id`. Define one canonical codec and its CIDv1 calculation. Bind `sha256` and `bytes` to the exact retained bytes, and the semantic CID to the decoded canonical object. Resolve `store_id` through trusted owner configuration; derive object locations from verified identities, with no caller-supplied arbitrary path or URL.

The signed child references the complete immutable parent and signed companion. Compact pending data references the complete signed child admission, receipt and graph, retaining the exact task specification, dependency CIDs, acceptance/output requirements and owner identity. Do not replace a signed object with an unverified digest or omit its population from replay.

Keep these four bindings explicit and equal throughout the manifest declaration, planning receipt/reference, pending contract and worker context:

- `parent_admission_cid`
- `signed_bridge_cid`
- `bridge_cid`
- `codebase_preview_successor_context_cid`

Define the context CID over the new canonical declaration without including its own CID. The companion continues to bind the immutable parent, avoiding a child/companion identity cycle. Sign every reference and the complete closure identities in the enclosing contracts.

Acceptance checks:

- Every encoded task body remains at or below 262,144 bytes with the original tasks intact.
- References have exact field sets, approved codecs and bounded per-object bytes, aggregate closure bytes, member count and depth; cycles and duplicate conflicting identities reject.
- Wrong schema, missing members, truncated bytes, mismatched hashes/CIDs, foreign stores, symlinks and changed objects reject before mutation or process birth.
- Generic materialization still rejects preview worker profiles; only the dedicated current-owner route can authorize installation or execution.

## 4 Preserve replay and current owner checks at every boundary

Resolve and validate the complete bounded closure at materialization, public instruction replay, resource reservation, runtime construction, START and completion. Verify signatures, the immutable parent equality contract, full graph, exact task/dependency/acceptance/output population, pending identity and all four bindings every time. Pure replay reports historical consistency only.

At each executing boundary also reconstruct original materials and freshly observe native source/model heads, structural context, policies, capabilities, providers, configuration, task source and checker bytes. Finish reads, compilation and application callbacks before the final native close. Preserve detached custody checks, process/thread bindings, transaction rollback and the existing STOP/cancellation drain rules.

Reject downgrade attempts, mixed old/new schema pairs and inline/reference hybrids. A legacy reader encountering the new profile fails closed. Migration authors a new signed child over the same immutable parent; it does not rewrite retained admissions or silently reinterpret existing pending tasks. Maintain regression coverage for old profiles and explicit cross-version rejection controls.

## 5 Define reference lifetime and offline delivery

Write artifacts atomically into owner-controlled immutable storage before publishing references. Pin the transitive closure while any installed task, pending completion, live worker, restart cursor or retained lineage can require it. Record durable ownership and references so crash recovery cannot garbage-collect an active closure.

Offline workers receive the complete pinned closure and approved store mapping before reservation or START. Verify the supplied bytes locally on every load; a saved receipt is never a live owner capability. Missing offline members reject without implicit network retrieval. Cross-process handoff authenticates the new receiving operation rather than carrying a reusable freshness token.

Allow garbage collection only after owned work has stopped, pending tasks have reached their retained terminal policy, successor/rollback references are accounted for and the audit retention interval has elapsed. Test interrupted installation, restart, cancellation, failed completion and rollback so each preserves needed artifacts and releases only its own pins.

## 6 Qualify full scans and the new worker generation

After the compact profile passes controls, run a fresh full-300 installation, dispatch, START, authored validations, completion, cancellation and STOP campaign. Reuse no positive task-installation credit from the preview-only milestone. Publish a new native source generation after worker edits; keep its Git/source delta, model lineage and scan roots explicit.

Cover every page in default optimized and explicit opt-out modes, including cold scans and continuation in fresh processes. Compare complete source coverage, ordered page/cursor links, candidate identities, task mappings and numerical results under declared tolerances. Check add/remove/rename/change transitions, reject stale heads/cursors, and require no unrequested fitting during resume. Record process births and weights/optimizer identities before and after each continuation.

Proof-cache entries discharge obligations only through accepted checker results bound to the exact source generation, formal obligation, checker policy and proof inputs. Invalidate affected joins after source or policy changes and retain checked reproof evidence. Autoencoder latents and similarity scores support candidate selection and planning; they never discharge an obligation.

## Larger head and training gates

Qualify 768D and 4096D independently with explicit source-width, encoder/token/pooling contracts, task codecs and latent/model lineages. A larger body cap or a 4,096-element vector does not establish a trained 4096D head. Authentic Leanstral source embeddings remain a separate input lineage until its numerical adapters and training contract are qualified.

Each lineage needs complete CPU qualification and separate CUDA qualification where supported: actual device placement, optimized/opt-out equivalence, bounded memory, complete scans/restarts, signed dispatch and cleanup. Federated workers combine only compatible updates into one accepted model per lineage; later gradient synchronization through `ipfs_accelerate_py` and MCP++ remains a separately versioned milestone.

## Calibrate native expiry without changing authority or time

The host control establishes one real-clock native primitive boundary; it does not close native broker expiry. The captured broker has a **5-second ACK budget** and **95-second client request timeout**. The daemon's renewal interval is bounded by `min(lease_ms / 3000, 30 seconds)` with a 0.1-second floor; ordinary long leases therefore renew at up to 30-second intervals.

A native expiry fixture must calibrate those actual intervals, elapsed proof custody and lock ordering before execution. Use the authentic public same-fence renewal, retain the exact current lease supplied inside the transaction, and satisfy the full 5-second remaining budget immediately before prepared/send. Use the real clock and retain real renewal attempts, expiry, prepared/Popen/received-ACK ordering and post-check outcome. Do not disable the heartbeat, inject a clock or reuse a preliminary expiry that a genuine renewal could shorten or extend. Account for renewal blocked by the held native transaction/file lock; require bounded release and join, with no coordinator/server/catalog lock cycle. An expired post-check after possible birth must preserve birth/effect uncertainty and cleanup obligations, not fall into a zero-birth shortcut.
